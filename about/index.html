About

Jackson Kesecker

Roots

Rooted in Monterey County

Jackson was born in Monterey and raised in Soledad, in the heart of the Salinas Valley. He is an 11th-generation Monterey County resident, and his family's history here is part of why he does this work. From the beaches and views of the Monterey Peninsula to the agricultural fields of the valley floor, framed by mountain ranges on both sides, he knows this county as a neighbor and not just as a market.

Education

Educated for the Details

After graduating from Palma School in Salinas, Jackson attended Hartnell College, where he earned his Associate of Arts, then went on to Cal Poly San Luis Obispo for a Bachelor's degree in Political Science with a Pre-Law concentration and a minor in Music. That training shows up in his work: he reads contracts closely, asks the right questions, explains complicated terms in plain language, and advocates clearly for his clients at the negotiating table.

Mission

Why Jackson Does This

Selling a home is one of the biggest financial decisions you'll make. Jackson believes it deserves an agent who understands the local neighborhoods and economy, and who measures success by whether his clients reach their goals. His mission is to help his Central Coast community get the most from their homes, and to help the next generation of buyers find theirs.

He proudly serves clients in English and Spanish.

Process

What Working With Jackson Looks Like

If you're selling

  1. 01 A pricing strategy grounded in current local market data, so your home is positioned to attract strong offers from day one.
  2. 02 Preparation and marketing that show your home at its best, from staging advice to professional photography and a targeted listing launch.
  3. 03 Skilled negotiation of offers, terms, and contingencies, so you keep the leverage you've earned.
  4. 04 Clear communication through closing, so you're never left guessing.

If you're buying

  1. 01 A conversation first. Jackson learns your goals, timeline, and budget, and connects you with trusted lenders.
  2. 02 A focused search. You see homes that fit your needs, with honest input on price, condition, and neighborhood.
  3. 03 A confident offer. He guides you through pricing, negotiation, inspections, and contingencies.
  4. 04 A smooth close. He stays with you through paperwork and deadlines until you have the keys.
Personal

Away from work, Jackson has been playing guitar for over a decade, and his music minor was more than a side interest. He's engaged to his fiancée, and the two are building a life here on the Central Coast, which gives him a personal stake in the community he serves.
